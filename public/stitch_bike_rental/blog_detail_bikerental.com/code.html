THE HIGH PASSES OF SPITI:
A 10-DAY SOLO JOURNEY

WRITTEN BY

Tenzin Norbu

PUBLISHED

Sept 12, 2024

Home chevron_right Adventures chevron_right Spiti Journey

Into the Thin Air

The roar of the 411cc engine was the only companion I had as I crossed the Rohtang Pass. Below me, the lush valleys of Kullu disappeared, replaced by the stark, lunar landscapes of Lahaul. Spiti is not just a destination; it's a test of mettle, a dialogue between man and machine under the infinite blue of the Himalayan sky.

The ride from Kaza to Hikkim is where the altitude really begins to play with your senses. At over 4,400 meters, every breath is a conscious effort. Yet, the motorcycle performed flawlessly, its torque-rich delivery making short work of the steep, gravel-strewn inclines.

format_quote

"In the silence of the high passes, you don't find yourself; you create yourself. Every hairpin turn is a new chapter in the book of the wild."

The Kaza Connection

Arriving in Kaza felt like landing on another planet. The mud-brick houses, the prayer flags fluttering in the cold wind, and the warmth of the local tea shops offered a sharp contrast to the brutal cold outside. I spent two days here, acclimatizing and checking the chain tension before the final push toward the Kunzum Pass.
