OUR STORY

A Legacy of Adventure

Founded in the heart of Kathmandu, BIKERENTAL emerged from a singular obsession: to provide the most refined mechanical companions for the most challenging terrain on Earth. For over two decades, we have been the silent architects of countless trans-Himalayan expeditions.

What started with three vintage Royal Enfields in a small garage has evolved into Nepal's premier fleet of precision-tuned adventure machines. We don't just rent bikes; we provide the keys to the roof of the world, forged in ice and engineered for the ultimate ascent.
