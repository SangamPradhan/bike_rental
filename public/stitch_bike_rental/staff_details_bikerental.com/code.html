The Vision of the Summit

For Tenzin, the Himalayas aren't just a destination—they are a lived experience. Born and raised in the shadow of Ama Dablam, she brings a spiritual connection to the mechanical precision of adventure motorcycling. She has led over 40 high-altitude expeditions through the Mustang Valley and beyond.

Expert in navigating "liquid" conditions—where the road turns to mud, ice, or loose shale—she ensures every rider finds their peak performance without compromising safety in the thin mountain air.
